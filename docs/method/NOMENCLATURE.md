# Nomenclature and claim boundaries

Preferred terms:

- **Directional Expected Value of Perspective (EVoP):** current-information expected regret from choosing under one perspective and evaluating under another.
- **Per-draw perspective-regret diagnostic:** draw-varying diagnostic; not the primary policy quantity.
- **Perspective Acceptability Frontier (PAF):** acceptability probabilities plus the expected-value upper envelope across declared perspectives or mixtures.
- **Perspective regime discovery:** clustering and sensitivity analysis that identifies recurring economic/clinical decision regimes.

Avoid:

- “societal bonus” as a synonym for EVoP;
- “true perspective” or “true welfare standard” without an explicitly declared normative assumption;
- “wrong decision” where the evaluative standard is contested;
- “empirical demonstration” without an evidence ledger and clear statement that the model is empirically parameterised simulation;
- treating perspective choice as if it were ordinary empirical parameter uncertainty.
