# vop_poc_nz product definition

## Vision

Provide a reproducible Aotearoa New Zealand empirical and tutorial compendium
for societal-perspective health-economic analysis while contributing portable
Value of Perspective contracts and fixtures to VOIAGE.

## Ownership boundary

- VOP owns case studies, evidence metadata, reproducible pipelines, policy
  interpretation, and the canonical VOP-VOIAGE compatibility contract.
- VOIAGE owns general-purpose calculation APIs, backend implementations,
  registries, and cross-language bindings.
- Repositories interoperate through versioned schemas, Arrow artifacts,
  deterministic fixtures, and adapters; neither imports the other's source.

## Excellence criteria

Public analysis inputs and outputs are typed, immutable, validated, provenance
bearing, backend explicit, numerically reproducible, and migration compatible.
Concerns, assumptions, risks, decisions, evidence and issue links are
machine-readable. Experimental features are capability-gated and never imply
unsupported maturity.
