# C06 — Preprint v2 and publication strategy

**Repositories:** vop_poc_nz, voiage

**Depends on:** C01, C02, C03, C04, C05

## Objective

Update the arXiv software/application preprint, prepare the Value in Health methods paper, and keep an NZ-specific housing paper distinct without duplicating claims.

## Deliverables

- preprint delta plan
- reviewer response matrix
- CHEERS/VOI checklist
- Value in Health method-paper outline
- NZ housing-paper boundary

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Headline concepts are limited to EVoP, PAF, and regime discovery.
- [ ] Claims use current outputs and evidence ledgers.
- [ ] The methods and NZ papers have non-overlapping primary questions.

## Agent operating rule

Map the live worktree and inspect existing implementations before editing. Prefer the smallest compatible change, record evidence in the local track state, and never promote local/private artifacts implicitly.
