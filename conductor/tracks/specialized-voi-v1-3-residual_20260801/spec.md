# Specialized VOI v1.3.0 residual-frontier governance

## Overview

C18 is a parallel v1.3.0 continuation of completed C16 and the canonical
successor wave after C17/M21. It governs the accepted residual families under
VOIAGE issue #318 without rewriting C17's finite additive-MCDA contract. VOP
owns canonical requirements and projections; VOIAGE owns numerical runtime
implementation and must retain experimental maturity until every promotion
gate is separately evidenced.

## Requirements

1. M22-M32 are versioned v1.3.0 requirements with explicit MoSCoW priority,
   estimand boundaries, units, conditioning, chronology, costs, policies and
   estimator or solver assurance.
2. Every family must be implemented test-first or receive a reviewed exclusion;
   adjacent helpers, names, schemas, issue state and mock-only surfaces are not
   implementation evidence.
3. Results must retain deterministic serialization, typed diagnostics,
   provenance, invalid/pathological cases and explicit Rust/Python/R/Julia/Mojo
   dispositions.
4. Cross-family reductions and interactions must be explicit: risk is valued
   through policies; forecasts through decisions; portfolios jointly;
   implementation and information without assumed independence; modelling
   uncertainty separately from information acquisition; event, sequential,
   social, heterogeneity and realized-sample quantities without relabelling
   classical aggregate VOI; sampling-acquisition harm remains distinct from
   ordinary cost, downstream decision harm and risk-sensitive clairvoyance.
5. GitHub hierarchy, Project 28 fields, Conductor plans, roadmap, schemas,
   fixtures, runtime discovery and documentation must agree before a positive
   support claim.
6. Projection synchronization remains bounded, registered, conflict-safe and
   unable to merge, close issues, release, promote maturity or approve science.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01:** Root and C18 MoSCoW requirements enumerate M22-M32 at v1.3.0 and
  trace each requirement to #318 and its native issue.
- **AC-02:** Mermaid designs show conditioning, decision, decomposition,
  interaction and fail-closed promotion boundaries.
- **AC-03:** Each family has strict schemas, normative and pathological
  fixtures, analytical/enumerable references and deterministic diagnostics, or
  a reviewed exclusion with migration guidance.
- **AC-04:** Accepted repository-owned implementations expose honest
  experimental Python/API/CLI/documentation/discovery surfaces and explicit
  language dispositions without duplicate numerical kernels.
- **AC-05:** A versioned projection and synchronization tests cover all native
  issues, nested delivery subissues, implementation PRs and Project 28 fields.
- **AC-06:** Focused, full local and hosted exact-head checks pass for every
  implementation and canonical-governance revision.
- **AC-07:** Scientific review, parity, stable promotion, release and issue
  closure remain pending unless separately evidenced and authorized.
- **AC-08:** M32 remains an unsupported research scope until a content-bound
  independent scientific/domain review and separate named human verdict approve
  the same narrow candidate; mathematical feasibility and positive ENBS never
  authorize a real study.

## External gates

- Independent scientific and terminology review for every positive estimand.
- Hosted exact-head validation in both canonical and runtime repositories.
- Shared-fixture Rust, R and Julia parity where advertised; Mojo remains external.
- Explicit stable-promotion, release and issue-closure decisions.
- For M32, candidate-bound independent review, a separate named human verdict
  and any applicable real-study ethics or regulatory authorization.

## Out of scope

- Numerical method implementation in VOP or VOP-to-VOIAGE source imports.
- Treating forecast accuracy, risk statistics, uptake multipliers, portfolio
  scores, subgroup summaries or sample-value uncertainty as VOI without the
  governed decision/conditioning contract.
- Assuming independent information sources or independent information and
  implementation effects by default.
- Automatic merge, closure, release, scientific approval or risk acceptance.
