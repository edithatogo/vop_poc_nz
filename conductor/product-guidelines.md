# Product guidelines

- Prefer explicit scientific meaning over convenience dictionaries.
- Preserve units, dimensions, perspective, population, currency year,
  uncertainty, provenance, and numerical policy at every public boundary.
- Fail closed on invalid or ambiguous scientific inputs.
- Keep local/private evidence local until explicitly promoted.
- Maintain backwards compatibility through documented adapters and warnings.
- Label experimental capabilities honestly and require parity evidence before
  promotion.
- Keep calculation kernels pure; orchestration, reporting, plotting and file
  writing belong at separate edges.
